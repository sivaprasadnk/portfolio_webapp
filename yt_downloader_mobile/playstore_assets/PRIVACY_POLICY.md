## 1. Overview
**YT Downloader** (`dev.sivaprasadnk.yt_downloader_mobile`) is an on-device mobile media utility for extracting and saving video streams, audio tracks, high-resolution thumbnails, closed-caption subtitles (`.vtt`), and metadata snapshots. We respect your privacy and designed the application so that **all media extraction, audio/video muxing, and library management happen locally on your device**.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We **do not** collect, store, log, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or downloaded media history on any developer-operated servers. No account registration or sign-in is required.

## 3. Device Permissions
YT Downloader requests only the permissions strictly necessary for core app functionality:
- **Internet (`android.permission.INTERNET`)**: Used directly on your device to fetch media metadata, download user-requested media streams, and check for app updates via Google Play In-App Updates or GitHub Releases.
- **Photos / Media / Storage (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 9 and below, `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` up to API 32, and scoped `MediaStore` write access on Android 10+)**: Used only when saving downloaded MP4 videos, JPEG cover images, audio tracks, or subtitles to your device's Photos/Gallery album (`YT Downloader`) and `Download/YT Downloader` folder.

## 4. External Link Sharing & Intents
When you share a link from an external application (such as YouTube or a browser) to YT Downloader via the Android Share Sheet, the shared URL is processed solely on your device to load the requested media information.

## 5. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
- The app contains **no third-party advertising SDKs**.
- The app contains **no behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs**.
- On Android, the app uses Google Play's official In-App Updates API (`com.google.android.play:app-update`) and checks public GitHub Releases (`api.github.com`) to notify you when a new version of the app is available.

## 6. Children's Privacy
YT Downloader does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
