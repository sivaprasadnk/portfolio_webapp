Privacy Policy for YT Downloader

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the YT Downloader mobile and web application (dev.sivaprasadnk.yt_downloader_mobile) developed and published by Sivaprasad NK.

1. Overview & Local Processing

YT Downloader is an on-device media utility for extracting and saving video streams, audio tracks, HD cover thumbnails, closed-caption subtitles (.vtt), and metadata snapshots. All URL parsing, stream extraction, audio/video muxing, and file management occur 100% locally on Your device.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or downloaded media records on any developer-operated server. No user account or sign-in is required to use the Application.

3. Device Permissions

  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used directly by Your device to fetch media metadata, download user-requested streams, and check for application updates via Google Play In-App Updates or GitHub Releases.
  • Photos / Media / Storage: Used solely when You save downloaded MP4 videos, JPEG cover art, audio tracks, or subtitles to Your device's Photos/Gallery album (YT Downloader) and Download/YT Downloader directory.

4. Third-Party Services & Updates

  • The Application contains no advertising SDKs and no behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • On Android, the Application uses Google Play In-App Updates and queries public GitHub Releases to check for newer versions on launch or when requested in Settings.

5. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
