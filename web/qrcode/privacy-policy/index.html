2. Collecting and Using Your Data

Personal Data

QR Code Studio does not require You to create an account and does not collect, store, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (such as Your name, phone number, email address, contacts, or precise GPS location) on external servers.

100% On-Device QR Generation & Scanning

All QR codes You generate (including text, URLs, custom patterns, corner eye styles, and color palettes) and all QR codes You scan (via live camera or uploaded images) are processed 100% locally on Your Device. None of the text, links, or images You enter or scan are ever uploaded or transmitted to any external server.

Device Permissions Used

To function reliably as a QR code generator and scanner utility, QR Code Studio may request the following standard Android/iOS permissions:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA / NSCameraUsageDescription): Used strictly to scan QR codes in real time through the in-app camera viewfinder and to control the camera torch/flashlight during scanning. Camera frames are analyzed locally in memory and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Photo Library / Media Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 9 and below, system photo picker / gallery access on Android 10+ and iOS): Used only when You explicitly tap Save to Gallery to save a generated 1024×1024 PNG QR image to Your Device, or when You tap Upload Image to decode a QR code from an image You select.

None of these permissions are used to collect personal information or track Your behavior.

Diagnostic & Usage Data

When You access the Application through a mobile device installed via Google Play, standard platform services (such as Google Play Services) may automatically collect basic, anonymized diagnostic and crash logs (such as device model, operating system version, and crash stack traces) solely to help Us fix bugs and improve app stability. You can review Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Use of Information

Any locally stored theme preferences or anonymized diagnostic logs are used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service: Generating high-resolution custom QR codes, saving PNG files to Your gallery, and decoding QR codes via camera or image selection on Your Device.
  • To improve stability and performance: Identifying and resolving crashes, camera compatibility issues, or layout problems across Android and iOS versions.
  • To respond to Your support requests: If You contact Us directly via email, We use Your email address only to reply to Your inquiry.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share Your personal data with third parties or advertisers. The Application contains no third-party advertising SDKs and no user-tracking SDKs. We may disclose information only if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

5. Retention and Deletion of Data

Because QR Code Studio processes QR content in memory on Your Device and does not maintain cloud accounts or remote databases, no personal data or QR history is retained by Us. Any QR images You explicitly save to Your Device gallery remain under Your full control and can be deleted at any time from Your gallery or by uninstalling the Application.

6. Security of Your Data

We value Your trust in using QR Code Studio. Since all QR encoding and decoding happens locally on Your Device within the operating system's application sandbox, unauthorized external access to Your QR content is prevented by design.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is a general-audience utility application and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data via email or support channels, please contact Us so We can promptly delete it.

8. Links to Other Websites

When You scan a QR code containing a web URL and tap Open URL, the link opens in Your Device's default browser at Your direction. We advise You to review the URL and the Privacy Policy of any third-party website You visit. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or practices of any third-party sites or services encoded in QR codes You scan.
