2. Collecting and Using Your Data

Personal Data

ChronoCraft does not require You to create an account and does not collect, store, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (such as Your name, phone number, email address, contacts, or precise GPS location) on external servers.

Locally Stored App Data

All clock configurations, alarms, alarm labels, timers, stopwatch laps, selected world clock time zones, and theme/display preferences are stored locally on Your Device. This data never leaves Your Device and can be cleared at any time by deleting items within the Application or clearing the Application's storage in Your Device settings.

Device Permissions Used

To function reliably as a clock, alarm, and timer utility, ChronoCraft may request the following standard Android/OS permissions:

  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Used strictly to trigger Your scheduled alarms and countdown timers at the exact time You specify.
  • Notifications & Full-Screen Intent (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT): Used to display active timer progress, upcoming alarm alerts, and full-screen alarm notifications when the Device screen is locked.
  • Foreground Service & Wake Lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE / WAKE_LOCK): Used to keep active timers, stopwatches, and ringing alarms running reliably in the background.
  • Receive Boot Completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Used to automatically reschedule Your enabled alarms after Your Device restarts.
  • Vibration & Audio Settings (VIBRATE / MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): Used to play alarm tones and vibration patterns when an alarm or timer goes off.

None of these permissions are used to collect personal information or track Your behavior.

Diagnostic & Usage Data

When You access the Application through a mobile device installed via Google Play, standard platform services (such as Google Play Services) may automatically collect basic, anonymized diagnostic and crash logs (such as device model, operating system version, and crash stack traces) solely to help Us fix bugs and improve app stability. You can review Google's privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
