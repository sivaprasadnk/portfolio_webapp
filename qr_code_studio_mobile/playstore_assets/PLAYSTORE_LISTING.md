## 3. Full Description *(4,000 characters max)*
```text
QR Code Studio is a clean, fast, and customizable QR code generator and scanner built for everyday use, developers, creators, and businesses. Create custom-styled QR codes in real time, export crisp 1024×1024 HD PNG images directly to your gallery, or scan any QR code using your live camera or photo library — with zero ads and zero tracking.

✨ GENERATE QR CODES INSTANTLY
• Type or paste any URL, website link, plain text, Wi-Fi info, contact details, or note.
• Real-time vector-sharp QR preview updates as you type.
• One-tap Paste and Clear controls for effortless workflow.

🎨 CUSTOMIZE YOUR QR STYLE
Make your QR codes stand out while staying 100% scannable:
• Pattern Styles: Choose between classic Square modules, modern Rounded modules, or sleek Dots.
• Corner Eye Shapes: Customize the three finder eyes independently with Square, Rounded, or Circle frames.
• Curated Color Palettes: Pick from 8 high-contrast foreground colors (Midnight, Sky Cyan, Indigo, Emerald, Violet, Rose, Amber, Teal) and 6 clean background tints.
• Error Correction Levels: Select L (7%), M (15%), Q (25%), or H (30%) reed-solomon error recovery for maximum scan reliability.

📸 FAST CAMERA & GALLERY SCANNER
• Live Camera Scanner: Instant QR detection with built-in Flashlight/Torch toggle and Front/Rear camera switch.
• Scan from Image: Pick any screenshot or saved photo from your device gallery to decode QR content on-device.
• Smart Actions: Copy decoded text to clipboard, open web URLs directly in your browser, or load any scanned QR straight into the Generator to customize and re-export.

💎 3 SIGNATURE VISUAL THEMES
Switch anytime between three crafted UI styles in both Dark and Light mode:
• Glass: Translucent frosted glassmorphism cards with subtle cyan accents.
• Bento: Clean, minimal structured surfaces for distraction-free focus.
• Cyber: High-contrast neon HUD borders with a tactical terminal grid.

💾 EXPORT & SHARE IN HD
• Save 1024×1024 high-resolution PNG files directly to your phone's photo gallery.
• Share QR images immediately to WhatsApp, Telegram, Email, Drive, or any app via the native share sheet.

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST & OFFLINE READY
• 100% On-Device Processing: Every QR code you generate or scan stays on your phone.
• Works completely offline — no internet connection required to create or scan QR codes.
• No advertisements, no analytics trackers, no subscriptions, and no account sign-up.
```

## 5. Google Play Console Declarations (Quick Reference)
- **Ads Declaration**: `No, my app does not contain ads`
- **App Access**: `All functionality is available without special access`
- **Target Audience**: `13+` (General utility app, not directed at children under 13)
- **Data Safety Section**:
  - *Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?* → **No**
  - Camera permission (`android.permission.CAMERA`) is used strictly on-device for live QR scanning; no camera frames or scanned data ever leave the device.
  - Photo/Media access is used only when saving generated QR PNGs to the device gallery or selecting a user-chosen image to decode.
