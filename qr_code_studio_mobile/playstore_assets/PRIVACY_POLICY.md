# Privacy Policy for QR Code Studio

**Effective Date:** November 2, 2026

**Privacy Policy URL:** https://sivaprasadnk.dev/qrcode/privacy-policy  

## 1. Overview
**QR Code Studio** is an offline-capable utility application for generating, customizing, and scanning QR codes. We respect your privacy and designed the app so that **all QR code generation and scanning happen 100% locally on your device**.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
We **do not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or scanned/generated QR code content on any external servers.

## 3. Device Permissions
QR Code Studio requests only the permissions strictly necessary for core app functionality:
- **Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`)**: Used exclusively to scan QR codes in real time through the in-app camera viewfinder. Camera frames are processed locally on your device and are never recorded or uploaded.
- **Photos / Media / Storage (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` on Android 9 and below, system photo picker on Android 10+)**: Used only when you explicitly tap **Save to Gallery** to save a generated QR code PNG image, or when you tap **Upload Image** to decode a QR code from a photo you select.

## 4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
- The app contains **no third-party advertising SDKs**.
- The app contains **no analytics or tracking SDKs**.
- When you tap **Open URL** on a scanned web link, the link is opened in your device's default browser at your direction.

## 5. Children's Privacy
QR Code Studio does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
